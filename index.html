UK Pension Allowance Rules Reference Guide

1. Standard Annual Allowance (AA)

For tax years 2023/24 onwards, the standard Annual Allowance is £60,000 per tax year. This limit includes personal contributions (grossed up), employer contributions, and Defined Benefit (DB) pension input amounts.

2. Tapered Annual Allowance (TAA)

High earners are subject to a reduced Annual Allowance. Tapering applies if both conditions are met:

  • Threshold Income: Exceeds £200,000 (taxable income minus personal pension contributions).
  • Adjusted Income: Exceeds £260,000 (taxable income plus employer pension contributions).

For every £2 of Adjusted Income above £260,000, your Annual Allowance reduces by £1. The minimum floor allowance is £10,000 (reached at £360,000+ adjusted income).

3. Money Purchase Annual Allowance (MPAA)

If you flexibly access Defined Contribution (DC) pension benefits, your allowance for future DC contributions is reduced to £10,000. Carry forward cannot be used against the MPAA limit once triggered.

4. Carry Forward Rules & Order of Set-Off

You can carry forward unused allowance from up to 3 previous tax years provided you were a member of a registered pension scheme in those years.

HMRC Ordering Rule: Current tax year allowance must be fully used FIRST. Any excess contribution is then absorbed by the oldest available carry-forward year first (e.g., 2023/24 before 2024/25).
